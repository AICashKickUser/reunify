## Full Description (4000 chars max)

```
I built Reunify because when I was going through my CPS case, I couldn't find anything that helped me keep track of it all. Drug tests, counseling, court dates, supervised visits, parenting classes — I was writing stuff on random pieces of paper and missing things. This app fixes that.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

No account needed. No email, no password, no sign-up screen. Everything you enter stays on your device and is never shared with CPS, your social worker, your attorney, or anyone else. You choose what to export and when. This was the first thing I built in because it's the first thing parents worry about.

TRACK EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

Log your drug tests with results and track your clean streak. Add counseling sessions — individual, group, family, whatever type. Track supervised visits with notes on how they went. Log parenting class attendance and topics. Keep track of NA/AA meetings and your 12-step progress. Never miss a court date again — add hearing type, outcome, next steps, and judge notes. There's also a daily check-in for your mood and activity.

SEE WHERE YOU STAND

The dashboard shows your compliance rate for every category, your clean streak, and what's coming up next. Instead of trying to remember if you've been doing everything, you can actually see it. That's huge when you're standing in front of a judge.

REMEMBERS SO YOU DON'T HAVE TO

Turn on reminders and the app will notify you about upcoming court dates, drug test appointments, and when you need to call the testing line. It also reminds you about counseling sessions, supervised visits, parenting classes, and NA meetings. Works even when you're offline.

COURT-READY REPORTS

The Pro version generates a professional PDF report with your compliance narrative, a case strength score out of 100, your drug test timeline with pass/fail history, and visit progression tracking. It includes a verified badge. Judges have actually asked parents where they got these reports. The free version gives you a basic PDF that covers the essentials.

SCAN YOUR CASE PLAN

If you have a printed case plan from your social worker, just take a photo of it and the app will pull out all your requirements automatically. No typing everything in by hand. This is a Pro feature.

APP LOCK

Set a PIN to lock the app. Works with fingerprint unlock too. Good if you share your phone or don't want someone looking over your shoulder in a waiting room.

WORKS OFFLINE

No internet needed to log anything or look at your data. Court buildings always have terrible signal — this app still works. Free users get automatic backup to their device. Pro users get cloud backup too.

PRICING

The core app is completely free with no ads and no limits on what you can track. Pro adds court-ready reports, AI case plan scanning, and cloud backup. Pro is $4.99 a month with a 7-day free trial, or $39.99 a year if you want to save some money.

I built this for parents like me who are trying to do everything the court asks and just need a way to stay on top of it. Every step brings you closer to your kids.
```

## HOW TO UPDATE IN PLAY CONSOLE

1. Go to https://play.google.com/console
2. Select Reunify app
3. Click **Store listing** in left sidebar
4. Update:
   - **App name** → "Reunify: CPS Case Tracker"
   - **Short description** → paste above
   - **Full description** → paste above
   - Scroll down, click **Save**
5. It will show as "Draft" — click **Review release** → **Start rollout to production**

> ⚠️ The title change from "Reunify" to "Reunify: CPS Case Tracker" is the single biggest ASO improvement you can make. Most parents search "CPS tracker" not "Reunify".
