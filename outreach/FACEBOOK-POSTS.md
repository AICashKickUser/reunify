## ⚠️ FACEBOOK GROUP RULES

1. **Read the group rules first** — some groups don't allow links. If that's the case, just say "search Reunify on Google Play" without the link.

2. **Don't post in multiple groups on the same day** — Facebook will flag it as spam. Post in 1-2 groups per day.

3. **Engage before posting** — spend 10 minutes commenting on other people's posts in the group first. Groups kick out people who join and immediately drop a link.

4. **Reply to every comment** — when people respond, engage. That's how posts go viral in Facebook groups.

5. **Don't share across groups** — write a fresh post for each group, don't share the same post. Facebook's algorithm punishes shared posts.
